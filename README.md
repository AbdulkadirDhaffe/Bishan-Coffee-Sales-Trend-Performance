# ☕ Baydan Coffee Shop — Sales Trend & Performance Analysis

## 📊 Project Overview

**Bidhaan Coffee Sales Performance Dashboard** is a practical Data Analytics and Business Intelligence project designed to analyze coffee shop sales performance from **January to June 2024**.

The dashboard transforms transactional sales data into clear business insights, allowing management to monitor overall sales, order volume, product performance, branch performance, customer payment behavior, and cashier performance.

This project was developed as part of my practical **Data Analytics learning and bootcamp experience**, where I applied data analysis and dashboard development skills to a realistic coffee shop business scenario.

---

## 🎯 Business Objective

The main objective of this project is to provide a clear view of Bidhaan Coffee's sales performance and help answer key business questions:

* How are sales changing month by month?
* Which branch generates the highest sales?
* Which products perform best?
* Which menu categories contribute most to sales?
* Which payment methods are most commonly used?
* Which cashiers generate the highest sales?

The analysis helps transform operational sales data into information that can support **performance monitoring and better business decisions**.

## 📈 Dashboard Analysis

The dashboard contains several views designed to monitor different aspects of business performance.

### 1. Monthly Sales Trend

Tracks sales performance from January through June and helps identify periods of stronger and weaker revenue performance.

### 2. Sales by Branch

Compares revenue generated by each branch.

**Taleh** recorded the highest sales at approximately **$2,425**, followed by **Banaadir Mall at $2,221**.

### 3. Top 5 Best-Selling Products

Highlights the products generating the strongest sales.

The leading products shown in the dashboard include:

* Grilled Chicken Panini — **$858**
* Lotus Tres Leches — **$770**
* Cappuccino — **$735**
* Lotus Cheesecake — **$690**
* Chicken Wrap — **$690**

### 4. Sales by Category

Compares sales contribution across menu categories.

**Cakes** generated the highest category sales at approximately **$2,096**, followed by **Salads & Sandwiches at $1,902** and **Coffee at $1,752**.

### 5. Payment Method Distribution

Shows the distribution of orders across different payment methods.

**EVC Plus** was the most frequently used payment method, representing approximately **53%** of the distribution.

### 6. Top Cashier Performance

Compares sales performance among the highest-performing cashiers.

The dashboard highlights **Asha Mohamed** and **Ahmed Ali** as the two leading cashiers by sales.

---

## 💡 Key Insights

The dashboard provides several important business observations:

* **Taleh** was the strongest-performing branch during the analyzed period.
* **Cakes** represented the highest-performing menu category by sales.
* **Grilled Chicken Panini** was the top-selling product in the dashboard.
* **EVC Plus** was the dominant payment method, accounting for approximately **53%** of the distribution.
* Sales performance varied across branches, categories, products, and cashiers, providing opportunities for management to investigate the drivers of stronger performance.
